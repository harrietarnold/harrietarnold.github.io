BEHIND THE SHOW

Wunderhorse's performance at Thekla Bristol in this converted ship venue was a unique experience. The intimate setting with the warehouse atmosphere created incredible visual opportunities. The industrial backdrop contrasted beautifully with the emotional rawness of the performance. I loved how the space itself became a character in the photos—the metal structures, the ship's interior architecture, all framed around the band's intense delivery. This shoot reinforced how much the venue influences the story we tell through photography.
