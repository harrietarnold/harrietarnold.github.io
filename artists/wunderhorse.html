BEHIND THE SHOW

Capturing The Hives at Debaser Strand in Stockholm was an absolute thrill. Their high-energy performance and dynamic stage presence created perfect moments for concert photography. The precision and intensity of their playing meant there was constant visual movement—every gesture deliberate, every moment electric. The Swedish venue provided a perfect intimate setting that amplified the raw power of their performance. This show is a masterclass in capturing movement and emotion in real-time.
