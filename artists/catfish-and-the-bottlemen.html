BEHIND THE SHOW

Catfish and the Bottlemen's set at the O2 Academy Brixton was a high-octane affair. The band came alive on stage with infectious energy, and I worked to capture the interplay between the band members as much as their connection with the crowd. The lighting was challenging—heavy blues and purples that required careful exposure balancing—but these atmospheric conditions created some of my favorite images from the night. This show reinforced my belief that the best concert photos aren't always about the biggest moments, but about reading the room and capturing authentic emotion.
