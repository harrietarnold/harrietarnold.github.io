BEHIND THE SHOW

Inhaler's set at The Rescue Rooms in Nottingham was a portrait of youthful exuberance. The band's confidence and technical prowess were obvious, but what struck me most was the genuine joy in their performance. The underground venue created an intimate, almost claustrophobic energy that the band channeled into their sound. I focused on capturing the interplay between band members, the connection with the front row, and the raw emotion in Eli's vocals. This show reminded me why emerging artists often deliver some of the most authentic and moving performances.
