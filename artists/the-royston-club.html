BEHIND THE SHOW

The Royston Club at the Roundhouse was a masterclass in intimate performance at scale. Despite the venue's size, the band managed to create an atmosphere that felt personal and deeply moving. I found myself drawn to the quieter moments—the drummer's focused expression, the bassist's subtle head nods, the audience members lost in their own emotional landscapes. These are the shots that define my work: not the flashy moments, but the genuine human connection that happens when music brings people together.
